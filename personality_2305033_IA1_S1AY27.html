Large Language Models(LLMs) Impact?

"LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch.” I strongly believe that this statement is true. Individuals tend to rely soley on artificial intellegence whenever they are tasked, regardless of it level of difficulty that the task portrays. AI limits critical thinking when used excessively but whenever used responsibly, Ai can help to organize data and break down complex topics. The video below explain means by which AI can be used ethically.

Additionally, LLMs can be used to improve students learning abilty. It can create fun and interactive actitities that are prefered by students, at the pace at which they are able to learn. This is what I personally do. I ask certain AI models to create quizes, riddles, stories and songs using my pdfs whenever i'm studying for upcoming exams. This method has worked in my favor. I realize that the information is memorized better this way. I also use it to explain complex maths topics and to help with the detection of errors in my codes whenever I cant figure it out. I also ensure to further verify my answers, as AI tends to give misinformation at times.

Whenever AI is used in excess, there can be serious consequences. Frequently depending on AI without attempting to complete given tasks on your ownwill lead to lack of brain development which limits critical thinking, causes laziness and even critical situations like academic dishonesty and expulsion from school.
